Copy-paste source for the store listings. Update this file when the listing

## App name (30 char limit)

    Momentum Energy Dashboard

## Short description (80 char limit)

    Turn your Momentum usage export into readable daily, weekly and hourly charts.

## Full description (4000 char limit)

Your Momentum Energy usage, finally readable.

Download your usage file from the MyAccount portal, open it in the app, and get
clean half-hourly charts of what you used and what it cost — by day, by week,
and hour by hour.

WHAT YOU GET
• Data — your whole export at a glance, with usage and cost totals
• Days — every day in the file; tap one for a half-hourly breakdown
• Weeks — week-by-week totals
• Switch any view between Usage (kWh) and Cost ($)
• Colour-coded by tariff period: off-peak, shoulder, peak and controlled load
• Weekends billed off-peak, the way your retailer bills them
• Rotate to landscape for side-by-side charts
• Set your own rates in Settings — supply charge, peak, shoulder, off-peak and
  controlled load — so the dollar figures match your actual plan

HOW TO GET YOUR FILE
The app walks you through it the first time you open it:
1. Open My Usage in the Momentum Energy MyAccount portal (the app links you
   straight there)
2. Choose a time period and tap "Export table" — a few weeks works best
3. Tap Import my CSV and pick the downloaded file

Not a customer yet? Try it with the sample data first — it is clearly marked
as sample so you never mistake it for yours.

The file is read on your device and never leaves it. The app keeps it there so
your charts are waiting next time, until you remove it in Settings.

GOOD TO KNOW
This app is only useful to you if you are a Momentum Energy customer in
Australia. Both the older multi-meter export and the current single-meter export
are supported.

Note that the Momentum Energy MyAccount portal is a little unreliable, so if
your first attempt to download your usage file fails, change the dates and keep
trying. It works eventually.

Momentum Energy Dashboard is not affiliated with Momentum Energy other than that
we are a customer of their electricity services. The name Momentum Energy is
their trademark.

## What's new (500 char limit)

A step-by-step guide on first launch shows exactly where to download your usage
file in Momentum MyAccount and how to import it. Sample data is now optional
and always labelled as sample. Your imported file is remembered between
launches, and Settings can remove it. Chart bars now fill their space properly
on every screen size, and About shows the right version.
